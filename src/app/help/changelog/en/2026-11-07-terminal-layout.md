On wide screens, choose **Beside page** in the terminal header. You can switch and edit pages while the terminal stays visible. **Below page** restores the bottom layout. One remembers your preference; phones use the full-width view.

**Settings and tools** groups model selection, skills and other controls. **Task context** identifies the running task’s source; **Next request** shows which page will accompany your next input.

Reopening the terminal resumes your reading position in the current conversation. The background task bar offers separate actions to **Answer question**, **Review changes** or **View result**. It also lets you jump to current work while a task runs.

The activity display names observed reads and tool calls, shows elapsed time and distinguishes waiting for a tool, writing a response and waiting for your decision.
