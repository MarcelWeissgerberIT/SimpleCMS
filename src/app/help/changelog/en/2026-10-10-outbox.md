**Three steps.** ⌘ on a mirrored row → **Reply in the ticket tool**. Claude drafts the text in a request with no tools at all; the slip shows exactly what would go out — the raw text, the tool's inputs, what changes in One afterwards and what Claude read. You send it: **Copy & open**, post it, **Mark as sent**.

**Foreign text is marked.** Comments, mails and pages you did not write carry a **FOREIGN** mark. An address that only foreign text brought in needs your tick before the slip can go out; hidden characters show as `⟦U+202E⟧`.

**CHANGED per mirror.** When the next mirror run brings a new comment, the slip lights **CHANGED** and quotes it — <kbd>H</kbd> holds, <kbd>R</kbd> drafts again, **Send anyway** if it still fits.

**Receipts.** Marking a slip sent updates the row as one version, archives the answered inbox note and keeps a receipt — **Reset row** puts the row back. Everything stays in this browser; `#/outbox` shows all slips, today's receipts and every send route.

Send routes come from an [integration profile](help:integrations) (`sends`); their tools are switched off in every other Claude request. More in [Outbox](help:outbox).
