Every switch in One is now an **industrial toggle switch**: a lever on a small metal plate held by two screws, **O** and **I** engraved, and a lamp that lights up in your signal colour when it is on. The lever snaps over — or, with reduced motion, simply changes.

Nothing else changes: the same rows, the same names for screen readers, <kbd>Space</kbd> and <kbd>Enter</kbd> still switch, the focus ring stays visible. In **Carbon** the plate turns dark metal, and a workspace's [look](help:workspace-look) tints it along with everything else.
