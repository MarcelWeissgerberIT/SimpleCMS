**Set up AI.** One assistant does it all: pick the providers, add each key, choose the models, decide what may go where and who answers what. Open it with ⌘K **Set up AI**, from **Settings → AI**, or from any card that says AI isn't set up yet.

**OpenRouter.** One key for hundreds of models from many makers — some very cheap. **Connect account** asks you at OpenRouter whether One may get a key (you copy nothing and can set a spending limit right there), or paste one. The free check sends no text; a key is kept only once it passes.

**The Jev Router.** With two or more models in your list, the Jev Router (by TypeSafe, via OpenRouter) picks the right one for each request. The price is reported per request, and the receipt names the model it picked.

**Data rules per provider.** A new provider starts with nothing: choose **Everything**, **Work data** or **Marked only** — or switch single kinds. Claude keeps what it had.

The AI terminal, agents, scripts and MCP servers stay with Claude in this version. More in [AI providers](help:ai-providers).
