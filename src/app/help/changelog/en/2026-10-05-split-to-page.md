Mark the blocks, then **Turn into → Page** in the block menu ⋮⋮, **Ask AI → Structure → Turn into page**, or <kbd>Mod+Alt+9</kbd>. The blocks move into a new sub-page and one link stays in their place — no Claude involved.

- A leading heading becomes the new page's title; otherwise the first line does.
- Inline databases, linked sub-pages and comments inside move along.
- The toast's **Undo** — or <kbd>Mod+Z</kbd> while the new page is unchanged — puts the blocks back.

**Turn into database** has a new **Place** too: **Here (inline)** as before, or **As its own page (linked)** — a full-page database below this page, with a link where the list was.
