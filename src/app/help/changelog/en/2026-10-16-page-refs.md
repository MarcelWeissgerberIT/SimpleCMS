**@ in your answer.** When Claude Code asks, type **@** (or `one:`) in **Your answer** — the same page search as in a page — and pick with <kbd>↑</kbd> <kbd>↓</kbd> and <kbd>Enter</kbd>. The page shows as *@Title* and its text goes to Claude Code with this very run. It works in a rework note and in **New task** too, and a pasted page address turns into *@Title* by itself.

**Give the page.** When Claude's question names a page in quotes and a page here has exactly that title, a key under the question puts it into your answer. Several pages with that name are listed with where they live. Nothing is sent before you press **Answer**.

**Linked pages go along.** Claude Code now also reads the pages linked in the pages a task links — one level deeper, at most 8 pages in all. **Goes along** shows them live while you write; a page Claude sees named but does not get is named there, and Claude is told how to ask for it.

**Permalinks.** **Copy permalink** in the page's ⋯ menu, on the plate at the end of every page and in the side peek. Paste it into the address bar, into a page (it becomes a mention), into a coding answer, the AI terminal or ⌘K — ⌘K also opens a page from its id or the plate's short ID (*ID ABCD·1234*).
