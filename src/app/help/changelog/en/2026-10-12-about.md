**Settings → About** names the licences now: the app is MIT, the team server AGPL-3.0.

**Free stays free.** One in your browser costs nothing, and neither does running the team server yourself. If One saves you money, a donation is welcome — never required. A **Donate** key appears there once there is a place to give.

The hosted [team cloud](help:team-cloud) is in development; the help article says what it will cost.
