**The tour** — eight steps, each pinned to the real place with a thin frame: the slash menu, the AI menu, <kbd>Mod+K</kbd>, a database's views, the AI terminal, version history, a script's dry run, Help. **Do it for me** shows the step for real but small, on a practice page you can delete at the end; <kbd>←</kbd> <kbd>→</kbd> step, <kbd>Esc</kbd> ends. Steps that need Claude say so and show what would happen. A fresh workspace offers it on the Welcome page; <kbd>Mod+K</kbd> → **Start the tour** any time.

**What can One do?** — eighteen features in six groups (Write, Organise, Claude, Automate, Share & team, Capture). Each card says what it does and how to get there, its **Try it** key opens the real place, and **How it works** opens the article. Find it at the foot of the sidebar, in the workspace menu, in <kbd>Mod+K</kbd> and on top of the Help panel.

**A tidier AI menu** — the prompt comes first. Below it, only the actions used most for what you selected (text, several blocks, an image, a file, an empty line); **Translate**, **Transform into…** and **More…** open with <kbd>→</kbd>. Nothing is gone: **More…** has the rest, and typing finds every action. The block menu groups Claude's actions under one label and gained **Ask AI**; <kbd>Mod+K</kbd> lists its commands in groups. See [The AI menu](help:ai-menu).
