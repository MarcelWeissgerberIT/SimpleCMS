**Text size** — in **Settings → Appearance** and in **Workspace settings → Overview → Display**: a fader with four stops, **Standard** (the sizes you know), **M**, **L** and **XL**. Everything follows at once — menus, panels, the sidebar, pages, tables, the help. It is kept on this device: screens differ.

**Panels that fit.** The filter, sort and colour-rule panels never cut a rule off any more — when there is no room, a condition breaks onto a second line, and every panel stays inside the window. In the published app these panels were capped at a narrow width, so the right end of a rule could disappear; that is fixed.

**Drag them bigger.** Filter, Sort, Group, Properties, Layout, Colour rules, Sub-items, Dependencies, Record types and the AI menu have a small hatch at the bottom-right corner: drag it, or <kbd>Tab</kbd> to it and use the arrow keys (<kbd>Shift</kbd> for bigger steps). One remembers the size on this device; double-click the hatch to go back.
