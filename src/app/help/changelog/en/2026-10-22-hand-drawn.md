Type `/handdrawn` → **Hand-drawn**. Choose an editable **Diagram** or an **Illustration** from a connected MCP image service. Describe the drawing, optionally include the page’s permitted AI context, then review it before applying.

Diagrams work without AI when you edit their data yourself; AI can draft or revise them using your chosen model. Illustrations retain the request and optional local reference. Reference images are described by the selected AI model; the image service receives that description.

Insert the drawing as a block or use it as a cover. Existing content stays in place. Sketch styling stays with diagram exports, while workspace backups retain drawing requests and references. Normal data rules and budget controls apply.
