**Actual Atlas result** provides collapsed receipts with returned IDs, references, counts and statuses. ONE checks recognizable contradictions in Claude, Copilot and local model answers. After at most one tool-free correction, observed metadata remains available; contradictory structured results are withheld.

ONE-mediated Copilot/local Atlas calls require an observed target before reference-based individual mutations. Successful create/update calls are read back once within existing read permissions. Native external Claude calls do not receive this execution check. Help explains scope and limits; product review and human acceptance remain necessary.
