Choose **New local workspace…** in the workspace menu: start empty or open a **workspace package**. Existing workspaces remain available, including in separate windows. Content, appearance, agents, rules, histories and approvals belong to their workspace.

**Workspace settings → Data → Export workspace package** carries content and referenced attachments. Personal preferences, keys, connections and approvals stay on the device. Imported agents and automations are paused; webhook destinations are removed. Existing workspaces and connections are not replaced.

On a team server, copy the open local workspace into a new team workspace and work together. Personal connections stay on your own device. Roles and invitations continue to apply. Copied page links include their workspace selector.
