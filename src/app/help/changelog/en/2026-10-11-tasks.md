- **A task, not a checkbox.** Type `/task` or `[!]` and a space. The first line is the title; notes and a to-do list of steps go below it.
- **Status in one press.** Click the key — or <kbd>Mod+Enter</kbd> — to mark it done, <kbd>Mod+Alt+Enter</kbd> for in progress. <kbd>Enter</kbd> at the end of a title starts the next task.
- **Due date, people, blockers.** Use the chips under the title or <kbd>Alt+Enter</kbd>. A task waits while what it is blocked by is still open — finish a blocker and One tells you who can start now.
- **Reminders for the right people.** A due date can remind you; in a team workspace only the task's people are reminded (everyone when nobody is assigned). Tasks with a date show on the Agenda too.

The demo workspace has a “Launch plan” to try it on. More in [Tasks](help:tasks).
