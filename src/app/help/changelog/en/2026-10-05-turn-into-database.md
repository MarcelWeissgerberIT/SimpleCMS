Select a list, a table or a whole report → **Ask AI** → **Turn into database**. Claude reads the blocks and proposes a table: entries with their fields — status, assignee, tags, reference codes — grouped by the headings they stood under.

- **Preview first:** the counts, the columns (switch off what you don't want), **Group by**, **Board** or **Table**, and what stays as text. **Convert** with <kbd>Enter</kbd>; <kbd>Mod+Z</kbd> brings the text back in one step.
- **Nothing is invented:** a value the text doesn't state stays empty.

AI requests also keep running when you close the panel or open another page — only **Stop** and **Discard** end them. The page shows *AI result ready · View*, the sidebar marks the page with a dot, and a toast with **Open** tells you when it is done. Results wait on this device, also after a reload.
