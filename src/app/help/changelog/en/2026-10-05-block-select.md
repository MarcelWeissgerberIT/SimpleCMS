Select blocks and they stay visibly selected: the **⋮⋮** grip and **+** stay pinned next to the first one (no hover needed), every block gets an orange wash — images and embeds too — and a chip counts them: **3 BLOCKS · ESC**.

- <kbd>Shift</kbd>-click another block, or <kbd>Esc</kbd> on a line and then <kbd>Shift+↑</kbd> / <kbd>Shift+↓</kbd>.
- Drag in the empty margin left of the text; <kbd>Mod+A</kbd> twice selects every block. <kbd>Esc</kbd> goes back to typing.
- On a phone: hold the grip, then tap the other blocks.

The grip, <kbd>Enter</kbd>, <kbd>Alt+Enter</kbd> or a right-click opens **one menu for all of them**: Turn into, Colour, Turn into page or database, Redo with instructions…, Duplicate, Move to, Delete. Each is one step for <kbd>Mod+Z</kbd>, and dragging the grip moves the whole range.
