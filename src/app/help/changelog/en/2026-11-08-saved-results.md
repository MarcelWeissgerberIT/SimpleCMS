Open **Work overview → Saved results** to find accepted analyses, saved mock previews and accepted code proposals. Search by result title, source page or saved status.

**Show more saved results** reveals the next twelve entries. Previously the overview counted older results but did not offer a way to reach them after the first twelve. Opening a result takes you to its original block and accepted version. Searching does not start AI or Atlas requests. A mock may perform its usual source check when opened.
