**Workspace settings** — from the workspace menu at the top of the sidebar, <kbd>Mod+K</kbd> → *Workspace settings* or *People*, or the link at the top of **Settings** — gather what belongs to the workspace:

- **Overview** — the name (it moved here from Settings), the numbers, what this device stores, the last full backup.
- **People** — everyone you can pick in a person property, with **where they are used** (click for the rows and pages). **Merge** a duplicate into someone and every value and @mention follows — one **Undo** puts it all back. Rename, recolour, add, remove someone nobody uses. Team workspaces: the members with their roles, the invites, and the people without an account.
- **Building blocks**, **Automation** (agents, scripts, functions, automations, commands, repeating entries, templates, the coding worker — on/off and last run, each with a way to it) and **Data** (backup, export, import, trash, sync), then the **Danger zone**.

**Settings** (<kbd>Mod+,</kbd>) is now *this device* and you: language, theme, the Claude key, sync, mail, MCP.
