The AI terminal now offers **Start your One memory** when none exists. **Try remembering a sentence** fills the prompt with an editable example. Submitting creates a local proposal; only **Save** stores it.

After a first completed task, **Suggest memories from this task** makes one additional AI request on your press. Suggestions remain yours to review. Once memory is set up, the existing automatic proposals take over according to your settings.

**Not now** dismisses the offer for this open terminal without changing your settings. The offer respects disabled memory, explicitly disabled proposals and read-only access. In a team workspace, your memory remains private.
