**A budget for all AI.** In **Workspace settings → AI § 06** set a day and a month budget for this workspace — each device measures it on its own. Your own limit for this browser, across every workspace in it, is in **Settings → AI**.

**At the red line One asks before it sends.** A request that would cross it is not sent: the note offers **+$1 once**, a cheaper model of the same provider, or Cancel. The [AI terminal](help:agent) waits between two steps with <kbd>E</kbd> · <kbd>H</kbd> · <kbd>Mod+.</kbd>. Agents never wait mid-run — they are held and say so once a day; autofill and mail pause until the budget rises.

**Read the usage.** ⌘K **Governor** (or the status bar's cell) opens `#/regler`: today against the budget, the month, who used what, and a receipt per run.

It is a pre-brake in One — set the hard limit with your provider too. Without a budget nothing changes. More in [Governor](help:governor).
