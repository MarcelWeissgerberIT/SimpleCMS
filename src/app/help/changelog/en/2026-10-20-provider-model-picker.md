A separate provider control now appears before the model in the AI menu, palette questions and Help Ask. Pick a usable provider, then a model from its filtered list. Ordinary model search still spans providers.

The pick applies to this request only and sends nothing until you start. Keys, data rules and capability checks remain in force; unavailable choices show why. Device defaults change only through the explicit default buttons. Coding engines retain their own selection.
