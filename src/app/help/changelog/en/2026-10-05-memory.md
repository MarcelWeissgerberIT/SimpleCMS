After a task in the AI terminal, Claude proposes what is worth keeping: **REMEMBER? · 2**. <kbd>y</kbd> saves, <kbd>e</kbd> edits, <kbd>n</kbd> dismisses — nothing is saved without your OK. Add your own with `/remember …`, *remember …* in the AI menu or **Remember this** on a selection.

The memory is a database in your workspace, **One memory** — facts, preferences, decisions and procedures with their template; private in a team workspace. Edit it like any database.

The matching memories go along with every free-form request — terminal, AI menu, ⌘K `?`, custom agents — as the template for the task. **MEMORY · 3** shows which, and the **Memory log** records every use: when, where, and which ones Claude cited.

**Pages as examples:** page ⋯ → **Save as example in memory**, give it a tag — then *“take #wochenbericht as the template”* builds new content in the same shape.
