The Preflight panel now uses recessed lamp lenses in a screwed charcoal housing. Grey hatching still means the check cannot run; a dark lamp means all right.

Lift the glass cover, then press **Master Caution** for new amber signals or **Master Warning** for new red signals. Opening a cover does not acknowledge anything. New or changed signals of that colour close it again. Tasks stay open after acknowledgement.

The compact master key opens the full panel for new red warnings. **Test** repeats the lamp check, respecting reduced motion and your lamp-test setting. Covers also work with the keyboard.
