**Quick capture**: <kbd>Mod+Shift+K</kbd> anywhere, the black **+** key at the bottom right on a phone, or **Quick note** when you long-press One's icon. Write (Markdown; the first line is the title), dictate with the **mic** (the browser's speech recognition, as in meeting notes), take a **photo** or attach a file, pick where it goes — **Clippings**, a database (a new entry) or the end of any page — and **Save**. It is stored on the device at once, offline too.

**Share to One** (Android, computer): installed, One takes photos, PDFs, audio and any file from the share sheet. It shows what arrived and asks before it saves to Clippings, then offers the next step — **Describe** or **Read out text** for images, **Summarise** or **Extract tables** for a PDF.

**Install One** sits in **Settings → General** and the workspace menu; on iPhone it shows how to add One to the Home Screen. iOS has no share sheet for web apps — use quick capture or the bookmarklet there.
