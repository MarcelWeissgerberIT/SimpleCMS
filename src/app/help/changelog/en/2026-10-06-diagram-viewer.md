A diagram that is wider than the page no longer has to be read at 20 %. **Open large** — the ↗ key on a Mermaid diagram or a chart, its block menu, or a double-click on the drawing — shows it across the screen. It stays vector: text and lines are sharp at every zoom.

- **Zoom** with <kbd>+</kbd> <kbd>−</kbd>, <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> / <kbd>⌘</kbd> + wheel or a pinch around the pointer, or a double-click. <kbd>0</kbd> fits the whole diagram, <kbd>1</kbd> shows it at 100 %.
- **Move** by dragging, with the wheel or the arrow keys.
- **The minimap** in the corner shows the whole diagram and an orange frame for what you see — click a spot or drag the frame to go there. <kbd>M</kbd> hides it.
- **Download SVG** saves the drawing; <kbd>Esc</kbd> closes.

A diagram the column shrinks says so in its corner — **Scaled 45 % · Open**. Charts are drawn again for the large canvas, so their labels stay readable; a database's Chart view and a spreadsheet's charts open large too. On a phone the viewer fills the screen and zooms with two fingers.
