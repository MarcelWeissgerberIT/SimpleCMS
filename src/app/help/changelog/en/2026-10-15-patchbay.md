**Everything that runs by itself, on one page.** The sidebar row **Patchbay** (or ⌘K) shows agents, automations, scripts, autofill, MCP servers, sync and the coding pipeline as modules: sources on the left, processing in the middle, targets on the right, with cables for what starts, reads and writes what. **§ In plain words** says each strand in a sentence.

**Fuses.** Where content from outside (a mail, an MCP read, a webhook) reaches an AI that acts without review, private data is in reach and a way out has nobody in between, the path turns orange and gets a fuse — with three keys on its plate: take the AI off "direct", cut the trigger or the write tools, or **Allow this path** (owners and admins in a team). Every key has Undo. Your sidebar, status bar and Preflight may show a fuse after this update: switched-off agents count (one press runs them), and a server nobody tested here counts with all its tools.

**Set up a profile** by dropping its `.integration.json` on the rack: lamps 00–06 lead through key, server, test, profile, database, agent and first run, and a chip in the top bar brings you back. **Live** shows this device's runs on the cables. The **Patching** switch lets you plug cables — never a new way out.

What changes elsewhere: on a locked database automations can only be switched off or removed; a field set to *Only by hand* shows its switch to everyone; the agent editor, its MCP tool list and Automations say *On fuse F1 — show in the Patchbay*; the agent editor and the mirror setup can open over the Patchbay; and its keys may change an agent's write mode or tools, a field's *Only by hand*, autofill's automatic mode, mail sorting, the bridge's mode or a server's *All AI calls* (that one on this device only).
