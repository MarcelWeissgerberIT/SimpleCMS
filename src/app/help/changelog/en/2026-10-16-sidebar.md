**Four places on top.** Search, Home, Inbox and Today stay rows (the Outbox joins once it is in use here). Everything else — Agenda, Graph, Agents, Scripts, Patchbay, Building blocks, Templates, Import — is one row of keys under **TOOLS**. The line above the keys names the key you point at and says what waits: *PATCHBAY · 1 OPEN FUSE*, the agents' proposals, a *NEW*. **NAMES** brings back the labelled rows; on an iPad or a phone the keys show their names.

**Favourites, always in view.** **01 FAVORITES** now sits in the fixed part above the tree. Point at any page: a **☆** appears — one click and it is a favourite. Or drag the page into FAVORITES, onto its head or between two favourites; the page stays where it is in the tree. Sort by dragging, with <kbd>Alt+↑</kbd> / <kbd>Alt+↓</kbd> or **•••** → **Move up** / **Move down** — this device keeps the order. Removing one says so, and **Undo** puts it back in its place. The page's **•••** menu has the star too.

**A calmer tree.** A hairline separates the fixed part from the tree; section heads stay on top while their rows scroll, and the list rests on whole rows. Opening a page shows its top-level page first under the head, so a journal day comes with its journal. **Collapse all** in the head of PAGES folds the tree back — the way to the open page stays open. The orange mark sits on one row only. Trash and **What can One do?** share one line at the bottom.

Fixed on the way: a favourite inside a page in the trash no longer numbers an empty section (FAVORITES always reads 01; restore that page and the favourite is back). More in [Sidebar](help:sidebar).
