title: Switch pages directly from the AI terminal
summary: Page search is available inside the terminal while your task keeps running.
help: agent
---
Use **Switch page** next to the terminal title to open page search and recent pages. The terminal folds away to make room for the page. Your task keeps running and remains accessible through **Return to task** at the bottom.

Opening another page from the sidebar or **⌘K / Ctrl+K** also folds the terminal away. Links within the same page leave it open. Task context and unsent input are preserved.
