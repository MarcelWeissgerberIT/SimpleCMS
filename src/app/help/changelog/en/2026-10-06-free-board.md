Type **/free board** in a page (or **+ → New free board** in the sidebar, or **Free board** under **+ Add view**). Its lanes are yours: **+ Lane** adds one, **•••** renames, recolours, moves or deletes it — a lane with cards asks where they go.

- **+** in a lane offers your **record types** ("Lead", "Bug", "Idea"), **Plain card** and **New record type…**. Each card shows its type's colour bar and its own fields.
- **Record types in every database**: **••• → Record types** attaches, detaches or creates them (also from existing properties). A row page shows **● Lead ▾** under its title; other types' fields stay hidden. Tables get an optional **Type** column to filter, sort and group by.
- **Turn into free board** in the AI menu: select mixed notes — Claude proposes the types (reusing yours by name), lanes and cards; you see a preview, one **Undo** takes it back.

Drag cards between lanes, or press <kbd>Space</kbd> on a card and use the arrow keys; on a phone, **Move to lane** in the card's menu. See [Free board and record types](help:free-board).
