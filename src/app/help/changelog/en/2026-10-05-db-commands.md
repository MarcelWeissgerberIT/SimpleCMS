Hover a database in the sidebar and click the **⌘** key next to **+**: its commands, one click away. The same list opens from the **⌘** key in the database's toolbar, on top of its right-click menu, on a phone under **⋯**, and in ⌘K — type *Mails: Sync now*.

- Defaults appear where they fit: **New entry**, **From template**, **Open view**, **Import CSV…**, **Export CSV**, **Copy link** — on the Mails database **Sync now** with the time of the last sync, and **Run "<agent>" now** for the custom agents that watch a database.
- **Edit commands…** switches defaults off and changes the order (drag, or <kbd>Alt+↑</kbd> / <kbd>Alt+↓</kbd>).
- **Add command**: button actions (an entry with preset values, a webhook, a link …), one of your agents, or a view.

A running command lights the LED on its key; the result comes as a short message, with **Retry** when something failed. A locked database keeps its list — its commands still run.
