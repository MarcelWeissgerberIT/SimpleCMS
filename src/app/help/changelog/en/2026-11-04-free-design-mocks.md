In **UI mock from Atlas**, enter a design MD link and your request, such as “Build a dialog for choosing a CyBio head”. A requirement record is optional.

ONE downloads large design MDs in full through your personal Atlas connection. The model receives selected original passages with design values and relevant components. Selecting a reference does not imply approval or a binding version decision.

You can adopt the preview into the block or download it. You only need a destination record when attaching the result to Atlas. Enter its global number from the `/r/…` permalink on the result card.

Local worker version 1.35.7 is available; the prepared cloud connection does not require a local worker.
