One reads Gmail straight from your browser, with **your own Google client ID** — a one-time setup of about five minutes that **Settings → Mail** walks you through. No server sits in between, and One only ever reads (`gmail.readonly`).

1. Paste your client ID and **Connect Gmail**.
2. Choose the day to start from, the labels, and when to sync.
3. The first sync creates the **Mails** database: subject, sender, date, labels, unread, a Gmail link — and the mail itself as the page. Remote images and tracking pixels stay blocked.

Switch on **Organise with Claude** and each mail gets a category, a priority, *Needs reply* and a one-line summary. Mail content reaches Anthropic only while it is on.

> Tip: the custom agent recipe *Daily mail triage* goes through new mail every morning.
