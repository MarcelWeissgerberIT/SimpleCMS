**Workspace settings → Look** sets how One looks for everyone in this workspace: the **signal colour** (keys, focus ring, cursor, the lamps of the switches), the **paper** and the **ink**, the fonts for the interface, page text and headings, and the corners.

Start from a preset — **Paper** (One as it ships), **Blueprint**, **Ochre**, **Proof**, **Swiss** — then change what you like. The whole tab previews your draft; **Save** applies it, **Discard** and **Reset to standard** take it back.

One keeps every colour readable (WCAG AA) in **Paper** and **Carbon**. Exports, shared links, the published website and the help pages keep One's standard look. In a team, owners and admins set the look; on one device you can still switch to the standard look — see [Your workspace's look](help:workspace-look).
