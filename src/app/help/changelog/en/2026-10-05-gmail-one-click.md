**Settings → Mail → Connect Gmail** — that's it. One brings its own Google access, so the five-step Google Cloud setup is gone. Google asks once which account to use and warns that it hasn't verified the app yet: click **Continue**. While Google reviews One, only approved accounts can connect; your own Google client stays available under **Use your own Google client (advanced)** and always takes precedence.

**Names instead of numbers.** The sync fills three linked databases — **Contacts** (by address, named from the sender line), **Companies** (by domain; gmail.com and other freemail never) and **Conversations** (one per thread, named by its subject without *Re:*). Each mail shows all three. Rename an entry once and every mail follows; **Merge…** joins two entries, with Undo.

**Attachments on demand.** Each attachment has a **Load** key: the file comes into the mail's page as a real block — a PDF in the viewer, an image, audio, video or a file to download. Or let new mails bring their PDFs and images right away with **Load attachments automatically**. HTML and SVG attachments are only ever a download.
