**Team hub** in the sidebar collects knowledge reviews, team areas, connected search, calendars and company sign-in. On a shared page, assign an owner and review date, then verify the exact current version. Later changes and expired reviews appear in the overview and the responsible person’s device inbox while One is open.

Open, closed and private team areas are separate workspaces with independent membership. Connected search combines One pages with explicitly selected MCP search sources, respecting the connection’s read permissions and data rules.

Database forms can publish a revocable public link. Visitors submit directly into One without signing in; they cannot browse existing responses. Publishing again replaces the old link. Unsupported question types are explained before publication.

Company sign-in supports Google and Microsoft through configured OpenID Connect providers; owners can issue scoped SCIM provisioning tokens. Personal Google or Microsoft calendars can show upcoming events read-only. These connections require administrator configuration and personal consent; without it, One shows setup information. Existing email sign-in remains available.
