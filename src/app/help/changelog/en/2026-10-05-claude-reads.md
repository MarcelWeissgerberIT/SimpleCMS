Under the prompt of the AI menu one line says what goes to Claude from this page: *Reads · whole page · 1,204 words*, *3 marked blocks · 412 words* or *nothing from this page*. Click it (or type *context*) to choose **Whole page**, **Only marked blocks**, **Mark blocks…** or **Nothing from this page**.

- **Mark blocks…** puts a box next to every block: a click or <kbd>Space</kbd> marks, <kbd>Shift</kbd>-click a range, <kbd>j</kbd> / <kbd>k</kbd> move, <kbd>a</kbd> all, <kbd>n</kbd> none, <kbd>Enter</kbd> done, <kbd>Esc</kbd> cancels.
- Unmarked text is never sent. With nothing to read, **Continue writing** and **Summarize this page** ask first.
- The same marks hold for `⌘K ?` and the AI terminal — its page chip shows the mode, `/context` opens the picker.

> Tip: marks belong to this tab — they follow your edits and are never saved or synced.
