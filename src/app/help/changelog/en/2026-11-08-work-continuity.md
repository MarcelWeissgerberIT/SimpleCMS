Mock note drafts now retain their text, selected elements or area, intent and scope across page changes and reloads in the same browser tab. They are private to your account and workspace for 24 hours. Closing the tab may remove them. Save the note to share it; when the mock changes, select its current targets again.

**Work overview** remembers the saved-results search and expansion when you return. **Work paths** groups the recorded sources, open steps and saved result of each block, with a direct next-step link. **Evidence links** can filter sources needing review and shows affected requirements as well as findings. These are recorded checks, not automatic claims that a changed snapshot changed a particular file.

From a code-analysis finding, choose **Create linked review task**. Its subpage offers the normal task assignee, date and comments, plus space for questions and the decision with rationale. A backlink opens the exact finding and revision. Completing the task does not silently accept an analysis.

**Budget before starting** shows the central service’s shared monthly budget, known spending, reservations and availability to its owner. Other members retain their existing visibility. The terminal’s estimate remains a guide for known text, not the price of an entire run.
