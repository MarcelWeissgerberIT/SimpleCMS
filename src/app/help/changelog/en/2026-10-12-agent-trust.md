**Discard with a reason.** In an agent's review the proposal with the focus takes keys: **↵** or **A** apply, **D** discards, and **1** discards it as **wrong** (in German also **F**, in English also **W**), **2** not now, **3** duplicate, **4** handled already. The same keys work on agent cards in [Preflight](help:preflight). **J / K** move, **Z** undoes a decision right in its row.

**Only *wrong* counts.** Not now, duplicate and handled already say nothing against the agent. Applying a proposal on its own counts as right — unless you change that value yourself within ten minutes. **Apply all**, **Discard all** and ⇧A count nothing.

**A meter per field.** Beside the runs, **Trust per field** shows the last 20 verdicts of each field as segments — green applied, hollow edited, raised orange wrong — with a line that says how far it is: *20 in a row*, *3 × wrong · 1 × edited*, *often reworded by you*.

On a touch screen **Discard ▾** opens the four reasons. Verdicts stay on this device. More in [Trust per field](help:agent-trust).
