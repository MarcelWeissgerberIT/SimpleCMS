The **Back** button is now visible at the top left of One. It returns to the previous page or area, including Agents, Coding and Patchbay. On a phone it appears as a left arrow.

It is disabled when this tab has no previous One step. Reloading keeps the available steps; browser Back and Forward continue to work. The main-column button in stacked panes remains a separate control.
