Press <kbd>?</kbd> (or **? Help** in the status bar) and the manual opens beside your page: 11 chapters, 54 articles, numbered like a technical manual. Settings carry small **?** keys that open the matching article.

- **Search** finds articles in both languages — *Formel* finds *Formulas*. The same articles show up in <kbd>Mod+K</kbd>.
- **Ask:** type a question and Claude answers from the help articles only — no page of your workspace goes along.
- **Keys:** <kbd>Mod+/</kbd> shows every keyboard shortcut.

The same manual is public at **/help** — handy to send a link. And this page, **What's new**, lists every update with a picture.

> Tip: <kbd>Alt+←</kbd> and <kbd>Alt+→</kbd> go back and forward inside the help, like a browser.
