**Ask AI → Redo with instructions…** (also in the block menu ⋮⋮, or `/redo` in the AI terminal). The picker opens with the selected blocks marked; mark more, then <kbd>Enter</kbd>. Write what should change — *shorter, informal, explain the jargon* — and keep it as a **preset** for next time. With `@` you can add a **rules page**, a style guide or glossary that goes along.

**Redo** (<kbd>Mod+Enter</kbd>) runs in the background like any AI request. The review shows one passage at a time — removed words struck through, new ones underlined:

- <kbd>y</kbd> or <kbd>Enter</kbd> accepts, <kbd>n</kbd> rejects, <kbd>j</kbd> / <kbd>k</kbd> move, <kbd>a</kbd> accepts all.
- Applying writes every accepted passage in one step — one <kbd>Mod+Z</kbd> takes it back, and a version is saved first.
- Formatting, links and mentions stay; a passage you changed meanwhile is skipped, not overwritten.
