**Every switch is now a glass rocker** in a small brushed-steel bezel held by four screws: the left half is engraved **0**, the right half **I**. The rocker tips toward the side that is pressed. Switched on, an LED under the glass lights it up and the gap around it glows; switched off, the glass stays milky white (smoked grey in Carbon).

**Choose the light.** **Settings → Appearance → Switch LED** — also in **Workspace settings → Overview → Display** — sets the colour every switch lights up in: the **primary colour** (the default: your workspace's signal colour, so it follows the [look](help:workspace-look)) or green, blue, yellow, red or purple. The choice stays on this device.

**Grows with the text.** Switches follow the **text size** now (Standard, M, L, XL), so their rows keep their proportions at every step.

Nothing else changes: the same names for screen readers, <kbd>Space</kbd> and <kbd>Enter</kbd> still switch, the focus ring stays visible — and with reduced motion the rocker simply changes.
