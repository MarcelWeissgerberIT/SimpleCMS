Choosing **Invite people** from Share now brings the invitation form into view and places keyboard focus on its mode switch, including on phones.

When you sign in for the first time from the default local workspace, One opens your personal space. An explicitly chosen local workspace stays local. Registration links keep their **Open my space** step; team invitations still lead to the invited workspace.
