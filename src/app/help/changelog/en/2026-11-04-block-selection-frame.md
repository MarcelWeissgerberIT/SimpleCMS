title: Select blocks without tinting their contents
summary: A fine frame replaces the orange overlay on selected blocks.
---

Selected blocks use a thin frame instead of a full orange overlay. Form fields, images, tables and charts keep their original colours and stay legible. The gutter rule, block grip and multi-selection count still show what is selected.

While editing a field, the outer frame becomes subtle and the active field keeps its focus ring. Text selection inside inputs stays visible. Selecting, moving, duplicating and keyboard controls work as before, including dark mode and small screens.
