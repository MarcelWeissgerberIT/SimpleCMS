Under **Workspace → People**, prepare welcome emails with personal recipients and separate team/guest destinations. A local parser assigns names, personal links and separate tokens. Saving sends nothing; only your click after the chosen time releases the messages.

After sign-in, **Start** offers a personal Atlas button. The modal opens only your own values; separate MCP tokens never appear in email. Confirm your successful connection test to dismiss your reminder. Protected access lasts seven days; durable connections belong in **My secrets**.
