Choose providers and models for one request: Claude, OpenRouter and the paired worker’s text models (local Ollama, Ollama Cloud, MiniMax and Codex). Provider availability gets its own line. Data rules stay effective and defaults stay unchanged. Requires worker 1.21+; text requests have no project or MCP tools.

After saving new provider rules, older One windows ask for a reload.
