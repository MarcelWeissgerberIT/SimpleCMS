**Filters as chips.** In <kbd>Mod+K</kbd>, type a filter and a space — it becomes a chip in the field, and the results follow every chip. Any property works by its name, in every database that has it: `status:done`, `priority:high`, `budget:>5000`. Then there are `in:projects` (the entries of a database, the pages below a page), `is:favorite`, `@alex` (someone in a person property), `by:sam` (who created or last edited it, in a team) and dates like `edited:7d` or `created:today`. A `-` in front negates; the same filter twice means either.

**Words go on top.** Type a word after the chips and it searches only what the filters found — titles and page text. Each result shows the values it was found by.

**Suggestions while you type.** `status:` lists the options with their number of entries, `@` the people, `in:` the databases and pages. <kbd>Tab</kbd> or <kbd>Enter</kbd> takes one, <kbd>Backspace</kbd> in the empty field removes the last chip. German keywords work too: `ist:favorit`, `geändert:7t`, `von:sam`.

**Recent and Frequent.** The empty field shows **Frequent** next to **Recent**: the pages this device opens most. The sidebar has both in one section under Favorites — switch with **Recent | Frequent** (or <kbd>←</kbd> <kbd>→</kbd>). The lists stay on this device: never synced, never in a backup.
