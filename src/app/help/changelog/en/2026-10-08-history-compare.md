**What is new, version by version.** In **Version history**, **Changes** now compares the selected version with **the previous version**: the words, blocks and property values it added are marked in orange, what it replaced is struck through on red. The banner names the comparison — *Compared with the previous version (Tue 10:42)*. The oldest version has nothing before it, so all of it counts as added; **Now** on the tape shows what changed since the newest version.

**Or until now.** **Compare → To now** shows, as before, everything that changed from that version until the page as it is now. One keeps your choice on this device.

**The version, marked.** **Version** shows the page as it was, in full, with what was new in it marked in orange — nothing struck through, nothing folded. **Mark what's new** switches the marking off.
