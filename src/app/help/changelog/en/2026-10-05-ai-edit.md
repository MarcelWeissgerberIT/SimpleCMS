Until now Claude could only add to a page. Ask the [AI terminal](help:agent) to **fix, rewrite, update or remove** something — *“The meeting moved to Tuesday, fix the notes”* — and it changes exactly those blocks, nothing else. Each edit is one proposal, shown as a **diff**: removed words struck through on red, new words in orange, unchanged blocks folded.

- Apply or discard each edit on its own. Applied edits of a page go in as one step after a version is kept — <kbd>Mod+Z</kbd> takes them back.
- A block you changed meanwhile is skipped, never overwritten; blocks you didn't let Claude read stay untouched.
- [Custom agents](help:custom-agents) can propose edits too — even one that writes directly waits for your OK before it replaces text.

[Version history](help:history) shows its changes word by word now, too, and a database entry's versions keep its **property values**: *Status: In progress → Done* above the text, and restoring brings them back.
