**Grips:** the **⋮⋮** grip and **+** of every block now sit in one column at the start of the line, left of the page — never on a bullet, number or checkbox. A nested list item is grabbed by the line it is on: point at *“Mix ½ well volume”* and you get that item, not its parent.

- **One grip at a time:** while blocks are selected, other blocks show no second grip. Hold <kbd>Shift</kbd> to extend the selection.
- Columns keep a grip at their edge.

**Footer:** every page ends with its footer — **Linked from** and the spec plate (created, edited, words, revision) — also on wide pages with the margin rail. The rail now holds the outline and upcoming reminders only; with neither, the text stays centred.

**Edited by:** in a team workspace the plate names who edited last — a person, an agent (*Agent · Wiki keeper*), the API or a webhook.
