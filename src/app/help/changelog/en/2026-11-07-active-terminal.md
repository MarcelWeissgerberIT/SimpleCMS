title: Keep your AI task visible while switching pages
summary: A compact task bar preserves the task, source context and result while you keep working.
help: agent
---
When you collapse the AI terminal, a compact **task bar** remains below the page. It shows the task and its original page context even while you work on another page. It reports **Your decision** when input is needed and **Result ready** after completion. One click returns to the current work, decision or answer without changing the open page.

The **current task** stays above the terminal history, with direct navigation to the task and result. Reading older entries pauses following; **New activity** takes you back to the current work and resumes following. Starting another task brings it into view. Previous proposals remain available through the existing review bar.
