Select a few blocks → **Ask AI** → **Transform into…** — or open the block menu ⋮⋮ of several selected blocks. Pick **Auto** and Claude chooses the form and says why, or pick one yourself: **Board**, **Table**, **Timeline**, **Diagram** (flowchart, mind map, sequence, Gantt, org chart), **Chart** (bar, line, area, donut), **Columns**, **Tabs**, **Toggles** or **Cards**.

- **Preview first:** the real result — the diagram drawn, the chart rendered. Switch forms with <kbd>←</kbd> / <kbd>→</kbd>; one asked before comes back without a new request. **Not carried over** shows what stays as text.
- **One step:** **Transform** (<kbd>Enter</kbd>) replaces the blocks; <kbd>Mod+Z</kbd> brings the text back. **Keep the original below** folds it into a toggle under the new block.
- **Nothing is invented:** labels, numbers and dates come only from your text — a number the text doesn't state is left out and listed.

Like every AI request, it keeps running in the background when you close the panel. See [The AI menu](help:ai-menu).
