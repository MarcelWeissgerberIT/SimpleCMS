- **Make tasks.** Select a few lines and choose **Make tasks** in the AI menu — or use it at the cursor for the whole page. Claude answers with tasks; you see each one with its owner and due date before it goes in. Meeting notes have a **Make tasks** key for their to-dos (no Claude needed).
- **The terminal and agents plan with tasks.** They write new tasks with owners, due dates and what waits for what, find tasks across the workspace and change their status, owners or links. Every change shows its tasks first — new, kept, changed before → after.
- **Nothing assigns itself.** Owners come only from the people of your workspace — One never creates a person. An agent that applies its changes by itself, or a client over the local MCP bridge with “Apply directly”, waits for you when a change assigns someone, sets a reminder, changes a task someone owns, adds a blocker or removes tasks.
- **Mentions stay mentions.** When Claude rewrites text, @-mentions of people and dates come back as mentions; a date keeps its reminder only when it had one.
