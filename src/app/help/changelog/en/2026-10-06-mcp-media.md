Image and video services that speak MCP now work in One end to end:

- **Save to One.** When Claude uses such a server, every picture, clip or sound it returns shows as a card — type, host, size — in the AI menu, in `⌘K ?`, in the AI terminal and in agent runs. Nothing loads until you click **Save to One**: the file is checked (type and content), stored in this browser and goes into the page. In the terminal it waits as **Insert media** for your review.
- **Generate.** Type `/generate image` or `/generate video` — or click **Generate…** in an empty image block. Pick the service, write the prompt, choose aspect ratio and count; Claude calls only that server and waits for the job. **Preview** a result, tick the ones you like, **Insert selected**. Generating may use credits on that service.
- **Sign in instead of a token.** Servers with their own sign-in page get a **Sign in** key in Settings → Claude AI → MCP servers. When the sign-in can't come back to One, **Sign in with a code** works the same way. The tokens are sealed in this browser — never in backups or exports.

A host that doesn't let browsers load its files gets **Open** and **Upload a copy**; in a team workspace also **Fetch through the team server**. More in [MCP servers](help:mcp-servers).
