Open a page and click **Share** (top right), or ⌘K → *Share page*.

The **Share link** contains the page itself, compressed into the URL. Anyone with the link gets a read-only copy of this version — later edits do not change it. Recipients can **Save to my workspace**.

1. **Copy** the link. The meter shows its size; very long links can be cut by chat apps or email — for big pages, send the HTML file instead.
2. Small images are carried inside the link; larger ones are left out (the dialog says how many).

## With a password
Switch on **Protect with password** and choose one (8 characters or more). The page is encrypted on this device (AES-256-GCM) before the link is made. The password is not part of the link — send it separately, e.g. by phone. There is no recovery: without the password nobody can open it.

## Other formats
- **Download as HTML** — one standalone file that opens anywhere.
- **Copy as Markdown** — for GitHub, Obsidian, READMEs.

> Share links never carry comments, button actions or live chart sources. In a team workspace, the dialog also offers to invite people — they edit live, with a role.
