## Quick capture
Press <kbd>Mod+Shift+K</kbd> anywhere. On a phone, tap the black **+** key at the bottom right; installed, long-press One's icon → **Quick note**. A small sheet opens:

- **Text** — Markdown works; the first line becomes the title. <kbd>Enter</kbd> saves, <kbd>Shift+Enter</kbd> starts a new line. On a phone the return key starts a new line — tap **Save**.
- **Mic** — a voice note: the browser's speech recognition (the one [meeting notes](help:meeting-notes) use) types what you say into the text. It needs a connection and is not in every browser.
- **Camera** — take a photo (on a computer: pick an image). **Paperclip** — attach any file. Up to 25 MB per file, 50 MB per capture.
- **To** — **Clippings** (a new page there), a **database** (a new entry, its title from the first line) or any **page** (added at its end).

Everything is saved on the device right away — offline too. The **+** key steps aside while you type.

## Share to One (Android, computer)
Install One first: **Settings → General → Install One**, or the workspace menu. Installed, One appears in the share sheet — share a web page, text, photos, PDFs, audio or any other file. One shows what arrived and asks **Save to Clippings?**: any app can share into One, so it never saves on its own. Files up to 25 MB each, 50 MB in all.

After saving, One offers the next step: for images **Describe** or **Read out text**, for a PDF **Summarise** or **Extract tables** ([Claude for images and files](help:ai-menu)). Offline, these wait until you are back online. A shared audio file stays a recording — transcription only works live, in meeting notes.

## iPhone & iPad
Safari gives web apps no place in the share sheet, so One cannot receive shares on iOS. Instead:
- quick capture in the [home-screen app](help:iphone) for notes and photos,
- the **Clip to One** bookmarklet (below) for web pages,
- or a Shortcut that opens `getonecms.com/app/#/clip?url=…&title=…&text=…` — One asks before it saves.

## Web clipper
**Settings → Data → Web clipper**: drag the **Clip to One** key to the bookmarks bar. On any page, one click saves the page and the text you selected to Clippings — without asking, because the key carries this device's own token.

> Long-press One's icon (Android, Windows, macOS) for **Quick note**, **New page**, **Search** and **AI terminal**.
