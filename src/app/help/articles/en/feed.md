**+** next to a database's views → **Feed** (or switch an existing view under **Layout…**).

Each entry becomes a card: icon and title, the properties you show as small spec labels, who edited it and when, the number of comments — and the page content itself, read-only. Long content is cut after a few lines: **Show more** expands it in place, **Show less** folds it again, **Open** opens the entry.

## Order
Newest first by default, by the time an entry was created. In **Layout…**:
- **Order by** — created time, or one of the database's date properties,
- **Order** — **Newest first** or **Oldest first** (the order switch above the cards reverses it too),
- **Show page content** — off for a compact list of cards.

As soon as the view has its own sorts, they decide the order.

## Writing
**New entry** adds a post and opens it. Filters, search and the properties shown work as in every view; a feed works in a page (inline database), in a full-page database and on a phone.
