**Workspace settings** gather what belongs to the workspace itself. Open them from the workspace menu at the top of the sidebar, with <kbd>Mod+K</kbd> → *Workspace settings* (or *People*), or from **Settings → Workspace settings** at the top of the list. **Settings** (<kbd>Mod+,</kbd>) keeps what belongs to *this device* and to you: language, theme, the Claude key, sync, mail, MCP, the coding worker.

The plate at the top reads the workspace: its name, **LOCAL** or **TEAM · 7 MEMBERS**, the number of pages and databases and what this browser stores.

## Overview
The **name** everyone sees (in a team workspace owners and admins rename it), the numbers — pages, databases, entries, words, people, own templates, the trash, since when — the storage this device uses, the files it keeps, and when this device last saved a **full backup**.

**Display · this device** holds the **text size** of the whole app: a fader with four stops — **Standard** (the sizes you know, and the smallest), **M**, **L** and **XL** (14 · 15 · 16 · 17 px for the interface text). Everything follows at once — menus, panels, the sidebar, pages, tables, the help. It is kept on this device only (screens differ) and is the same control as **Settings → Appearance**. The website and the pages you share or publish keep their own size.

## Look
The workspace's **colours, type and corners** — for everyone in it: a preset or your own signal, paper and ink colour, the interface, page text and heading fonts, standard or square corners. Contrast stays readable (One adjusts a colour that would fall short), and exports and shared links keep One's standard look. In a team workspace owners and admins set it. *Use the standard look on this device* is for this device only. See [The workspace look](help:workspace-look). The Overview shows the current look with a key to change it.

## People
Everyone you can pick in a person property or @mention. Each person shows **where they are used**: rows that hold them and pages that mention them — click the read-out for the list, each entry opens its page. *You* marks the local user (named like **Settings → General → Your name**).

- **Add** a person by name; a name can be taken only once.
- **⋯ → Rename** — the person's values and mentions show the new name.
- **⋯ → Colour** — their initials' colour everywhere.
- **⋯ → Merge into** another person — every person value and every @mention points at the other person, then the first leaves the list. A version of every rewritten page is kept, and **Undo** in the notice puts everything back at once.
- **⋯ → Remove** — only for someone nobody uses. Someone in use: merge them instead.

In a **team workspace** People lists the **members** with their roles (owners and admins change roles, remove members, transfer ownership), who has the workspace open right now, the **invites**, and below them the people without an account here — with the same tools for everyone who may write. Viewers read. See [Members & roles](help:members-roles) and [Invites](help:invites).

## Building blocks
How many shared lists, own property types and record types the workspace holds, with the way to each on the building blocks page.

## Automation
One read-only overview of what runs or is built to be reused: the coding worker of this device, agents, scripts, custom functions, database automations, own database commands, repeating entries and own templates — where each one sits, whether it is on, and its last run when this device knows it. Every line opens the place where it is made. In a team workspace the **API tokens and webhooks** are here too (owners and admins).

## Data
**Export workspace** (a full backup, Markdown, HTML, a website …) and **Import**, the last full backup from this device, the **trash** (open it right here), and where copies of the workspace go: the team server and this device's folder or GitHub sync.

## Danger zone
Local workspace: **Reset workspace** erases it on this device and starts fresh — export a backup first. Team workspace: the owner deletes it for everyone (type its name to confirm); everyone else **leaves** it. Removing a team workspace's copy from this browser is a device matter: **Settings → Data**.
