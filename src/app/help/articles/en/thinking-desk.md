Choose **Open thinking desk** on a page. Create a desk as a child page or select an existing one. **Add to desk** on an overview card carries that card into the desk.

## Work with cards
1. Open **Collect sources**, choose a page and drag useful passages onto the workspace. **Add to desk** is the keyboard and touch alternative.
2. Drag the statements you want to examine into the **Question area**, or use their checkboxes. Remove a selection with its × button.
3. Add a **question card**: **Contradictions**, **Open questions** or **Next steps**. The preview shows the specific question and how many statements it will use.
4. **Create thinking desk** creates a separate child page with those cards, their original sources and the question. The original desk remains available. Only **Answer this question** starts the AI in the new desk.

Question cards can also be clicked. You can also drop a question card onto the workspace. If nothing is selected, its preview includes all currently visible cards. On smaller screens the sources, workspace and question area stack vertically. To ask your own question directly, select cards and use the question field; remove a pending question card first if needed.

Clicking a statement opens its passage and source link. **Edit** opens its title, interpretation, group and evidence fields. Drag within the workspace to arrange cards; **Alt + arrow keys** is the keyboard alternative. New desks use 256 × 256 cards; **Display & filters** contains other sizes.

For heading-only sources, **Choose a supporting passage** returns to the source page. Suggested questions only fill the text field. After an answer, follow its source links to check the evidence.

## Sources and zoom
**Collect sources** offers passages, overview cards, attachment excerpts and saved Atlas code references. Attachments are first read locally as text; select a verbatim excerpt. Your own notes start as **Assumptions**. Sources must share the desk's private or shared visibility scope.

Six zoom levels run from **8 × 8** to **256 × 256 pixels**. Small dots show distribution; larger cards expose titles, interpretation and group. Click for the complete excerpt and source link. Drag or use **Alt + arrow keys** to arrange cards. Checkboxes select cards for analysis. Zoom is personal; cards and positions persist and synchronize with the team.

## Connections and decisions
Select two cards and **Connect cards** to record a possible conflict, support or dependency. **Compare & find conflicts** examines up to 20 selected cards. AI connections are possible conflicts requiring review.

Under **Decisions**, record what should apply. A confirmed decision can resolve a connection. Changes to its sources require renewed review.

**Propagate a decision** prepares edits to selected One pages. Review each **Before / Proposal**, then choose **Apply this change**. Only a unique, unchanged text passage is replaced. Files, code and passages spanning formatting boundaries need editing in their original source.

## Evidence and scenarios
Cards distinguish **Assumption**, **Source available**, **Code reviewed**, **Tested** and **Approved**. The last three require a rationale and evidence link, recording the person's confirmation and time. Assigning a level does not run tests or automatically open evidence links.

**What-if scenario** explores an alternative assumption. Save its result as a separate provisional plan with tasks. Original pages remain unchanged.

## Scope and limits
A desk supports up to 100 active cards. AI requests read only the selected cards and their declared excerpts. Atlas references are checked before sending code, and the repository must permit model processing. Normal data rules, model selection and budgets apply. Changed sources and cancelled runs do not become new saved results.

Full backups retain desks. Document-only exports do not reproduce the interactive card map. A single-page export does not include source pages outside its scope.
