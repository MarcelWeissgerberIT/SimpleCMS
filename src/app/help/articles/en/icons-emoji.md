## Page icon and cover
Hover the page title: **Add icon** and **Add cover** appear.
- The icon picker has **One icons** (ceramic objects), **Emoji** and **Symbols** (line glyphs), plus **Random**.
- **Add cover** offers a **Gallery** (One covers, patterns, colours), **Upload** (stays on this device) and **Link** (any image URL). **Reposition** lets you drag the image into place.

## In the text
- Type `:` and a word — `:rocket` — for emoji. `:thumbsup:` with both colons converts at once.
- `/emoji` opens the full picker: search, categories, skin tone; recently used ones first.
- `/icon` inserts a One icon or a line glyph sized to the line.
