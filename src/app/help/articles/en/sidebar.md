The sidebar lists **Search**, **Home**, **Inbox**, **Today** (your journal), **Agenda**, **Graph**, **Templates** and **Import**, then your pages.

## Pages
- **+** next to **Pages** creates a **New page**, a **New database** or opens the **Templates**.
- Hover a page and click **+** to **Add a page inside**. The arrow in front of it expands its subpages.
- Drag a page onto another to nest it, or between two to reorder. **Move to…** in its menu does the same from the keyboard.
- The **•••** menu of a row: rename, duplicate, add to favorites, copy link, move, open in a side pane or in the peek, delete.
- **Favorites** collects starred pages at the top.

## Databases in the tree
A database in the sidebar expands to its entries — in the order of its first view, at most 20, then **Show all · n** opens the database. Entries expand to their sub-items and sub-pages.
- **+** on a database row adds a **New entry**.
- Drag a page onto a database: One asks, then the page becomes an entry (its sub-pages come along; its properties start empty).
- Drag an entry out of its database: One asks before it becomes a normal page — its properties are dropped.

## Trash
Deleted pages go to **Trash** at the bottom of the sidebar. **Restore** puts a page back in its old place; **Delete forever** removes it from this device.

## Fold it away
<kbd>Mod+\</kbd> collapses the sidebar, and you can drag its edge to change the width. On a phone the sidebar is a drawer behind the menu button.
