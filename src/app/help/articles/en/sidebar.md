The sidebar has a fixed part on top and the page tree below it. A thin line separates them; the tree scrolls, the top stays.

## Places
**Search** (<kbd>Mod+K</kbd>), **Home**, **Inbox** (<kbd>G</kbd> <kbd>I</kbd>; an LED and a number while something is unread), the **Outbox** (once a send route is set up here or a slip waits — see [Outbox](help:outbox)) and **Today** (your journal).

## Tools
One row of keys under the places: **Agenda** · **Graph** · **Agents** · **Scripts** · **Patchbay** · **Building blocks**, then after a thin line the two that open a dialog: **Templates** · **Import** (not for viewers of a team workspace).
- The line above the keys says what waits most: open fuses (**PATCHBAY · 1 OPEN FUSE**), then the agents' proposals, then a **NEW**. With nothing waiting it reads **TOOLS**. Point at a key — or move the keyboard focus onto it — and the line names that key.
- A key keeps its LED and count in its corner: the Patchbay's fuses (a grey **?** when parts are unknown here), the agents' proposals (while [Preflight](help:preflight) is off — Preflight counts them while it is on).
- **NAMES** turns the keys back into labelled rows. This device remembers it.
- On a touch screen (an iPad, a phone) the keys show their names, two rows of four.
- Keyboard: the row is one <kbd>Tab</kbd> stop — the open tool's key, else the first. <kbd>←</kbd> <kbd>→</kbd> <kbd>Home</kbd> <kbd>End</kbd> move along, <kbd>Enter</kbd> opens.

Every tool is also in ⌘K.

## Favorites
**01 FAVORITES** sits in the fixed part, so it is always in view — also while the tree jumps to the open page. Empty, it says how to fill it.

Make a favourite:
- Point at a page in the tree: a **☆** appears on the right. Click it; the star fills.
- Or drag the page into **FAVORITES** — onto its head, its empty box, or between two favourites (a line shows where). The page stays where it is in the tree; a database entry stays in its database.
- Or the **☆** at the top right of every page, **Add to favorites** in a row's **•••** menu or in the page's **•••** menu, or ⌘K **Add page to favorites**.
- On a touch screen: the row's **•••** menu → **Add to favorites**.

Sort and remove:
- Drag a favourite up or down. Or focus it and press <kbd>Alt+↑</kbd> / <kbd>Alt+↓</kbd> — the focus stays on it and a screen reader hears its new place. Or **•••** → **Move up** / **Move down**.
- The order belongs to this device and this workspace. A favourite added elsewhere (another device, a backup) joins at the end, by title.
- Click the filled **☆** again to remove a favourite. A message says so; its **Undo** brings it back to its old place.
- A favourite expands like any page in the tree — a database to its entries. Its row shows where it lives (its parent page) after the title.
- **FAVORITES** shows up to five rows (four in a low window, three on a phone); more scroll inside the list. The arrow in its head folds it away; this device remembers.

A favourite inside a page that is in the trash is not shown. Restore that page and the favourite is back in its place.

## Pages
- **+** next to **Pages** creates a **New page**, a **New database**, a **New free board** or opens **Templates**. **Collapse all** (the key beside it) folds the tree back to its top level — the way to the open page stays open.
- Point at a page and click **+** to **Add a page inside**. The arrow in front of it expands it.
- Drag a page onto another to nest it, or between two to reorder. **Move to…** in its menu does the same from the keyboard.
- A row's **•••** menu (or a right-click, or <kbd>Shift+F10</kbd> on a focused row): rename (<kbd>F2</kbd>), duplicate, favourites, copy link, copy for the AI, move, open in a side pane (<kbd>Alt</kbd>-click) or in the peek, delete.
- The section heads (**02 RECENT | FREQUENT**, **03 PAGES**, **PRIVATE**) stay on top while their rows scroll by, and the list rests on whole rows. When you open a page, the tree shows it: its top-level page first under the head when the open page still fits below, else its parent. Nothing moves when the page is in view already, or in **FAVORITES**.
- The orange mark sits on one row only: the favourite's when the open page is a favourite, else the tree's.

## Recent & frequent
One compact section with a two-position switch: **Recent** lists the pages you opened last (the open one left out), **Frequent** the pages this device opens most — a page counts once it stayed open a moment, and coming back on another occasion counts again. The arrow at the right folds the section; the switch and the fold are remembered on this device (<kbd>←</kbd> <kbd>→</kbd> switch with the keyboard).
- Both lists belong to **this device only**: they are never synced, never in backups or exports, never sent to Claude.
- **•••** on the section head (or a right-click on it) → **Clear this list** or **Clear both lists**. ⌘K **Clear recent and frequent pages** does both.
- ⌘K shows the same two lists when its field is empty.

## Databases in the tree
A database in the sidebar expands to its entries — in the order of its first view, at most 20, then **Show all · n** opens the database. Entries expand to their sub-items and sub-pages.
- **+** on a database row adds a **New entry**.
- Drag a page onto a database: One asks, then the page becomes an entry (its sub-pages come along; its properties start empty).
- Drag an entry out of its database: One asks before it becomes a normal page — its properties are dropped.

## Keyboard
- <kbd>↑</kbd> <kbd>↓</kbd> <kbd>Home</kbd> <kbd>End</kbd> move through the favourites and the tree as one list; <kbd>→</kbd> expands or steps in, <kbd>←</kbd> folds or goes to the parent.
- <kbd>Alt+↑</kbd> / <kbd>Alt+↓</kbd> move a favourite; <kbd>Shift+F10</kbd> opens a row's menu; <kbd>F2</kbd> renames.
- The agenda's letter keys keep working while a key in the sidebar has the focus.

## Trash and "What can One do?"
The bottom line holds **Trash** — **Restore** puts a page back in its old place, **Delete forever** removes it from this device — and **What can One do?**, every feature on one page. In a narrow sidebar the latter shrinks to a compass.

## Fold it away
<kbd>Mod+\</kbd> collapses the sidebar, and you can drag its edge to change the width. On a phone the sidebar is a drawer behind the menu button.
