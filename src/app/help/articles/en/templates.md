## Built-in templates
**Templates** in the sidebar (or ⌘K → *Browse templates*) opens the gallery: **Built-in** templates by category — Work, Product, Personal, Knowledge. Pick one, choose **Insert into** (a page, or the top level) and click **Use template**. You get real, editable pages and databases with sample content.

## Your own templates
1. Open the page you want to reuse — subpages, databases and rows come along.
2. Page options **•••** (top right) → **Save as template…**
3. Give it a name, a description and a category, then **Save template**.

Your templates are under **Mine** in the gallery. Variables such as `{{date}}` or `{{user}}` are filled in each time a template is used. **Customise** turns a built-in into your own version.

## Row templates and repeating rows
In a database, the arrow next to **New** lists the database's templates: pre-filled properties and content for a new row. **New template** creates one.

In a row template, switch on **Repeat this template**: daily, weekdays, weekly, monthly or every N days, at a time, from a start date, optionally until an end date. The row title can use `{{date}}`, `{{weekday}}`, `{{week}}`, `{{time}}`, `{{name}}`.

> Repeating rows are created while One is open. After a break, One catches up at most 3 missed runs.
