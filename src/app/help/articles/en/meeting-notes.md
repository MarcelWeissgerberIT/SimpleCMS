1. Type `/meeting notes`.
2. **Record** — allow the microphone. The live transcript appears as people speak. **Pause** and **Resume** as needed; pick the **Recognition language**.
3. **Stop** — Claude writes the notes from the transcript: summary, decisions and action items, as normal blocks you can edit.

No live transcription in your browser (Firefox, some Safari versions)? **Paste a transcript** from Zoom, Google Meet or Teams — text or `.vtt` — and **Write notes**.

**Send action items to a database** turns the action items into rows, e.g. in Projects.

## Privacy
- Speech recognition is done by your browser: Chrome sends the audio to Google, Edge to Microsoft, Safari may send it to Apple.
- Only the transcript text goes to Claude, with your key. No audio is stored.

> A recording interrupted by closing or reloading the page can be resumed — or stopped to write the notes from what was captured.
