1. Select the words you want to talk about.
2. Click **Comment** in the toolbar, or press <kbd>Mod+Alt+M</kbd>.
3. Write and press <kbd>Enter</kbd> to send (<kbd>Shift+Enter</kbd> for a new line).

Comments sit in the margin next to their text. Click one to jump to the text; **Reply**, **Edit**, **Resolve** or **Delete thread** from its menu. Resolved threads can be re-opened. The top bar shows how many comments are open.

If the commented text is deleted, the thread stays, marked **Detached**.

> Comments are not part of share links, exports or published sites.
