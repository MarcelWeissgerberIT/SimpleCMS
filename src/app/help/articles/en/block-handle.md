Hover a block: a **+** and a **⋮⋮** handle appear on its left.

- **+** adds a block below (<kbd>Alt</kbd>-click: above) and opens the slash menu.
- **Drag ⋮⋮** to move the block — onto another place on the page, into a column, a toggle or a tab.
- **Click ⋮⋮** for the block menu: **Turn into**, **Colour**, **Copy link to block**, **Move to** (another page), **Copy and sync**, duplicate and delete. The menu has a search field: type `turn` or `red`.

## From the keyboard
- <kbd>Alt+Enter</kbd> opens the block menu of the block you are in.
- <kbd>Mod+D</kbd> duplicates, <kbd>Mod+Shift+↑</kbd> / <kbd>Mod+Shift+↓</kbd> move the block.
- <kbd>Esc</kbd> selects the whole block; then <kbd>Backspace</kbd> deletes it.

## Colours
**Colour** offers ten text colours and ten backgrounds (gray, brown, orange, yellow, green, blue, purple, pink, red). For words inside a line, select them and use the colour button of the toolbar.
