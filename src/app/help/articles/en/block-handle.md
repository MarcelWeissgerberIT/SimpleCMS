Hover a block: a **+** and a **⋮⋮** handle appear on its left.

- **+** adds a block below (<kbd>Alt</kbd>-click: above) and opens the slash menu.
- **Drag ⋮⋮** to move the block — onto another place on the page, into a column, a toggle or a tab.
- **Click ⋮⋮** for the block menu: **Turn into**, **Colour**, **Copy link to block**, **Move to** (another page), **Copy and sync**, duplicate and delete. The menu has a search field: type `turn` or `red`.

## Several blocks at once
Selected blocks keep their grip next to the first one (no hover needed), get an orange wash — images and embeds too — and a count chip (**3 BLOCKS · ESC**). Select them by:
- <kbd>Esc</kbd> on a line (selects its block), then <kbd>Shift+↑</kbd> / <kbd>Shift+↓</kbd> to grow or shrink;
- <kbd>Shift</kbd>-click on another block (or its ⋮⋮);
- a drag in the empty margin left of the text;
- <kbd>Mod+A</kbd> twice (every block);
- on a phone: hold the grip, then tap the other blocks.

The grip — a click, <kbd>Alt+Enter</kbd> or a right-click on a selected block — opens one menu for all of them: **Turn into**, **Colour**, **Turn into page**, **Turn into database…**, **Duplicate**, **Move to**, **Delete**. Each is one step for <kbd>Mod+Z</kbd>; dragging the grip moves them together. <kbd>Esc</kbd> goes back to typing.

## Turn into page
**Turn into → Page** (or <kbd>Mod+Alt+9</kbd>) moves the block — or the selected blocks — into a new sub-page and leaves one link in their place. A leading heading becomes the page's title, otherwise the first line does. Inline databases, sub-pages and comments inside go along; in a team workspace the new page is private exactly when this page is. The toast's **Undo** puts the blocks back and moves the new page to the trash; <kbd>Mod+Z</kbd> does the same while the new page is unchanged.

## Sub-page per item
A list, a few headings with text below them, or a table: **Turn into → Sub-page per item · 5 items** (on several selected blocks also **Transform into → Pages + table**) makes every item its own sub-page and puts one table in their place. A list item's first line is the page's title and its other lines and nested points are the page; a heading section becomes a page titled by the heading (text before the first heading stays); a table row becomes a page titled by its first cell, the other cells written as *Column: value*. The table links every page, plus up to three fields the items share — *Status: open*-style lines (status, owner, date and priority first), a to-do's checkbox as **Done**, or the table's next columns. Inline databases, sub-pages and comments inside go along; in a team workspace the pages are private exactly when this page is. **Undo** in the toast or <kbd>Mod+Z</kbd> brings the items back and moves the untouched pages to the trash.

## From the keyboard
- <kbd>Alt+Enter</kbd> opens the block menu of the block you are in.
- <kbd>Mod+D</kbd> duplicates, <kbd>Mod+Shift+↑</kbd> / <kbd>Mod+Shift+↓</kbd> move the block.
- <kbd>Esc</kbd> selects the whole block; then <kbd>Backspace</kbd> deletes it.

## Colours
**Colour** offers ten text colours and ten backgrounds (gray, brown, orange, yellow, green, blue, purple, pink, red). For words inside a line, select them and use the colour button of the toolbar.
