The buttons in the database toolbar belong to the current view.

## Filter
**Filter** → **Add filter rule**: property, condition, value. Dates compare against fixed days or relative ones — today, tomorrow, one week ago, one month from now. A person can be *me* — whoever looks at it. **Add filter group** nests rules with **And** / **Or**.

## Sort
**Sort** → **Add sort**, ascending or descending; several sorts apply in order. Without sorts, rows keep their manual order — drag them by the handle.

## Group
**Group** → **Group by** a property. Groups can be collapsed or hidden; **New row in this group** pre-fills the value. Boards always group.

## Search
The magnifier searches the rows of the view by title and properties.

## Which properties show
The sliders button (**Properties**) shows or hides properties in this view, or hides empty ones.

## Bigger panels
The panels of the toolbar never cut a rule off: when there is no room, a condition breaks onto a second line. Want more room? Drag the small hatch at a panel's **bottom-right corner** to make it bigger — Filter, Sort, Group, Properties, Layout, Colour rules, Sub-items, Dependencies, Record types and the AI menu have one. With the keyboard: <kbd>Tab</kbd> to the hatch, then the arrow keys (with <kbd>Shift</kbd> in bigger steps). The size is kept on this device for that kind of panel; double-click the hatch (or <kbd>Home</kbd>) to go back to the normal size. A panel is never smaller than it needs to be, never bigger than the screen; phones have no hatch.

> On a locked database, filters and sorts are yours only, for this tab.
