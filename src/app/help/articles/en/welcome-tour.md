On the first start One creates **Welcome to One** and a few examples: **Projects** (a database with board, timeline and chart), **Reading list**, **Content calendar**, **Team wiki**, meeting notes and **Budget 2026** (a spreadsheet with a chart).

The Welcome page has a **Quick tour** — a to-do list you can tick off: the slash menu, ⌘K, dragging blocks, the board, the spreadsheet, side-by-side panes, Claude, import and publishing.

## Getting rid of the samples
They are ordinary pages: delete them like any page (they go to **Trash**).

## Starting over
**Settings → Data → Reset workspace** deletes all pages, databases, files and history on this device and starts fresh — with the Welcome page again. Export a backup first if you want to keep anything.
