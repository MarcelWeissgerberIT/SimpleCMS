On the first start One creates **Welcome to One** and a few examples: **Projects** (a database with board, timeline and chart), **Reading list**, **Content calendar**, **Team wiki**, meeting notes and **Budget 2026** (a spreadsheet with a chart).

## The guided tour
On a fresh workspace the Welcome page offers **Take the 3-minute tour**. Eight steps, each pinned to the real place with a thin frame: type `/` for a block, select text → AI (Transform into), <kbd>Mod+K</kbd>, a database's views, the AI terminal (<kbd>Mod+J</kbd>), version history with its word-by-word changes, a script's dry run, Help and What's new.

- **Do it for me** does the step for you — real, but small: it types `/todo` on the practice page, opens the palette, switches a view, opens the history, runs a script as a dry run (nothing is written).
- <kbd>←</kbd> <kbd>→</kbd> move between steps, <kbd>Esc</kbd> or **×** ends the tour; on a phone the placard sits at the bottom.
- Steps that need Claude say so (*add your key in Settings → Claude AI*) and show what would happen instead of failing.
- The tour writes on its own page, **Tour — try things here**. At the end: **Delete the Tour page** (to the trash, with Undo) or keep it.

**Not now** hides the card for good on this device. The tour stays one step away: <kbd>Mod+K</kbd> → **Start the tour**, the top of the Help panel, or the workspace menu.

## The checklist
The Welcome page also has a **Quick tour** — a to-do list you can tick off: the slash menu, ⌘K, dragging blocks, the board, the spreadsheet, side-by-side panes, Claude, import and publishing.

## Getting rid of the samples
They are ordinary pages: delete them like any page (they go to **Trash**).

## Starting over
**Workspace settings → Danger zone → Reset workspace** deletes all pages, databases, files and history on this device and starts fresh — with the Welcome page again. Export a backup first if you want to keep anything.
