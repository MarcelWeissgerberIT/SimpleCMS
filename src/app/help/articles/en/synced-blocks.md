## Create one
- `/synced` inserts an empty **Synced block**: write into it.
- Or open the ⋮⋮ menu of any block and choose **Copy and sync**.

Then paste (<kbd>Mod+V</kbd>) on another page: the copy stays in sync with the original. A label shows how many pages use it.

## Manage
The block's menu (and the ⋮⋮ menu) offers:
- **Go to original** — from a copy,
- **Unsync** — this copy becomes normal blocks,
- **Unsync all** — on the original: every copy keeps its current content and stops syncing.

If the original is deleted, the copies keep their content, read-only, until you unsync them.

Synced blocks cannot contain synced blocks.
