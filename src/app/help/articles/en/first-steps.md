Open **getonecms.com/app/**. One starts with a Welcome page and a few sample pages and databases. Everything you create is stored in this browser (IndexedDB). There is no account, and nothing is uploaded.

1. Create a page with **+** next to **Pages** in the sidebar, or press <kbd>Mod+Alt+N</kbd>.
2. Type a title, press <kbd>Enter</kbd> and write. Type `/` on an empty line to insert any block — headings, to-dos, tables, databases, spreadsheets.
3. Press <kbd>Space</kbd> on an empty line to ask Claude (needs your own Claude key).
4. Press <kbd>Mod+K</kbd> to search every page and run any command.
5. Press <kbd>?</kbd> to open this help. All keys: [Keyboard shortcuts](help:keyboard-shortcuts).

## Settings
<kbd>Mod+,</kbd> opens **Settings** (also in the workspace menu at the top of the sidebar): your name, the language (English or Deutsch), the theme — light (Paper), dark (Carbon) or System — your Claude key and your data.

> Your workspace lives in this browser only. Export a backup now and then: **Settings → Data → Export workspace**.
