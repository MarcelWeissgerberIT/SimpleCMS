## Roles
- **Owner** — full control, including deleting the workspace.
- **Admin** — edits, and manages people and invites.
- **Member** — reads and edits pages and databases.
- **Viewer** — reads everything, changes nothing.

**Settings → Team → Members**: change a role, transfer ownership (*Owner (transfer)*), **Remove** someone. **Leave** takes you out yourself. Removing or leaving deletes that person's private pages in this workspace.

## Private pages
The sidebar's **Private** section holds pages only you can see — not even the owner or admins can open them; the server refuses.
- **+** in **Private** creates a private page or database.
- Drag a page into **Private**, or use **Move to Private** in its menu; **Move to** *workspace* makes it shared again.
- Everything below a private page is private too.

Moving between Private and the workspace needs a connection. Mails from **Gmail sync** always land in Private.
